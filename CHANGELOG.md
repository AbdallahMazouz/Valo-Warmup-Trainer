## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-23

### Added
- **10 training scenarios**: Flick, Smooth Tracking, Target Switching,
  Micro-Adjustments, Wide Swing, Jiggle Peek, Angle Clearing,
  Reaction Time, Counter-Strafe, Tactical Combo.
- **4 difficulty levels**: Easy, Medium, Hard, Valorant Pro.
- **Session statistics**: score, accuracy, hits, misses, avg reaction time.
- **Personal bests** tracked per scenario × difficulty combination.
- **Profile screen** with history overview and PB table.
- **Settings screen** with live crosshair preview and slider controls.
- **Animated main menu** with esports-inspired neon grid background.
- **Procedural audio engine** — no external sound files required.
- **Persistent saves** in `%APPDATA%/ValoWarmupTrainer/` (JSON).
- **Resizable window** and **F11 fullscreen** toggle.
- **pytest** unit tests for settings, stats, targets, and scenarios.
- `build_exe.py` PyInstaller build script for standalone Windows EXE.
- Full `README.md` and `LICENSE` (MIT).
